SOURCE ACCURACY AND PUBLICATION REVIEW
The supplied six-page technical whitepaper in assets/monkey_vr_whitepaper.pdf and its
embedded architecture figure were inspected. Claims were cross-checked against it;
see whitepaper-claims.txt for the page-level source map. Current Linux scope, Ghidra
models, cross-binary analysis, evidence review, original binary execution, observation,
validation contracts, interface fidelity, regression reuse, and fuzzing are supported
by the reference. This is source consistency review, not independent tooling validation.

The review tightened two points: RTOS and bare-metal images are outside current tooling
support, and execution mechanisms, reachability, and authentication require separate
evidence. RTOS and expanded bespoke cyber-physical emulation remain explicitly labeled
development directions requiring platform-specific engineering and feasibility scoping.
No completed RTOS demonstration or arbitrary-device full-system fidelity is claimed.

Technical detail requiring confirmation: model hosting, external API use, permitted
data transfers, and handling requirements. The paper mentions model calls and analyst-
controlled infrastructure but does not define these details. Current copy makes that
scoping requirement explicit and does not offer an all-local or provider-specific
handling assurance. Confirm actual behavior before adding any stronger public claim.

Demonstrated implementation behavior, modeled effects, and unverified consequences
remain separate. STPA-Sec informs IKELOS's broader workflow; no official-step or
scanner claim is made. No IKELOS ownership of underlying third-party tooling,
third-party developer identity, commercial details, customer outcomes, certifications,
guaranteed findings, or unattended autonomous verification are asserted. The source
PDF remains untracked; neither it nor its diagram is in the production build.
