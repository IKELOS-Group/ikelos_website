IKELOS — Section 02 research methodology refactor
Date: 2026-11-05
Branch: brand/systems-sigil-redesign
Local-only work. No push, merge, commit, or deployment.

Result
Replaced the five-tab "One system. Five ways to question it" interface with a
six-stage systems-engineering research workbench. Uses the requested title and
introduction verbatim. Preserves Systems Sigil, Steel Doctrine, existing routes,
contact/security contracts, and the independent scroll-driven narrative.

Implementation
- Six directly selectable stages with native buttons and aria-current="step":
  Mission & Consequence; System Modeling; Causal & Attack Analysis; Technical
  Investigation; Exploit Validation; Engineering Resolution.
- One explicitly fictional remote thermal-control module. A persistent control
  structure distinguishes operator authority, maintenance configuration,
  controller/process model, pump drive, thermal circuit, and temperature/flow
  feedback. Control and service boundaries, directed relationships, and the
  closed feedback loop are drawn explicitly. Mobile preserves that topology.
- Stage-dependent mission envelope, candidate causal-path overlays, research
  focus, evidence acquisition markers, and proposed control barriers. Labels,
  dashed paths, double outlines, and captions supplement color. No fake data,
  results, telemetry, exploit success, or completed remediation is displayed.
- Selectable components expose their assumptions, technical questions, and
  evidence requirements. A text topology description supports screen readers.
- Traceable analysis: L-01 mission availability; H-01 insufficient cooling;
  SC-01 adequate cooling constraint; M-01 control model; A-01 configuration
  authority assumption; ICA-01 insufficient pump demand in the specified
  operating context; CS-01 candidate calibration/process-model causal scenario;
  Q-01..03 targeted implementation questions; E-01..04 evidence requirements;
  D-01 engineering decision and V-01 verification.
- Validation separates access/authority, implementation effect, control effect,
  and operational consequence. The evidence chain supports component inspection.
  Negative/inconclusive results and insufficient access are explicit outcomes.
- Engineering Resolution offers a real return to System Modeling. All stages
  can be reopened directly; research is not presented as a completion checklist.
- STPA-Sec's role in systems analysis is explicit; the six stages are IKELOS's
  broader workflow, not official STPA-Sec steps. Attack hypotheses, modeled
  consequences, experimental findings, and proposed controls remain distinct.

Content basis
The supplied six-stage brief defines the methodology. Technical terminology was
checked against the MIT-hosted STPA-Sec paper:
https://web.mit.edu/smadnick/www/wp/2018-12.pdf
The thermal scenario, component assumptions, trace identifiers, questions, and
visualization are original illustrative material, not results from that paper
or an IKELOS engagement. No new organizational credentials or specialties added.
